# Famagusta Walled City & Ancient Salamis Route
*A journey transitioning from the grand ancient Roman ruins of Salamis to the medieval stone gates of Famagusta, ending with a bike ride through the silent streets of the Varosha Ghost Town.*

## Route Overview
* **Total Driving & Cycling Distance**: 19 km
* **Total Driving Time**: 30 minutes
* **Total Estimated Trip Duration**: 9.5 hours (09:00 AM – 06:30 PM)
* **Best Season**: Year-round (Spring and Autumn offer comfortable walking temperatures; Summer is ideal for swimming at Varosha Beach)
* **Recommended Vehicle**: Any standard rental car
* **Difficulty**: Easy (flat roads, easy highway navigation, and mostly pedestrian areas)
* **Family Friendly**: Yes (families will love cycling through Varosha and swimming at the beach)
* **Accessibility Notes**: **Salamis Ruins** is a flat archaeological site, but the paths are sandy, gravelly, and have ancient stone thresholds, which can make wheelchair traversal difficult without assistance. **Famagusta Walled City** is relatively flat and easy to walk. **Varosha (Kapalı Maraş)** features newly paved asphalt roads, making it exceptionally accessible for wheelchairs and strollers, though Varosha Beach is sandy with standard boardwalk access.

## Recommended Route

### 09:00
**Salamis Harabeleri (Salamis Ruins)**

A magnificent ancient Greek and Roman city-state. Stroll among marble columns, explore the amphitheater, bathhouses, and ancient mosaics. Founded according to legend by Teucer after the Trojan War. Under Roman rule, it became the island's primary commercial port. Starting here at 09:00 AM is essential to beat the scorching midday heat, as the site is completely open with very little shade.

* **Category**: Historic Site
* **Coordinates**: 35.1842, 33.9011
* **Google Maps**: [View on Google Maps](https://maps.google.com/?q=Salamis+Ancient+Ruins,+Famagusta)

**Recommended Stay**:

### 12:15
**Lala Mustafa Paşa Mosque (St. Nicholas Cathedral)**

The largest medieval building in Famagusta, featuring majestic Gothic architecture inspired by the Reims Cathedral in France. Built by the Lusignans between 1298 and 1312, it was where the Kings of Cyprus were crowned as Kings of Jerusalem. Converted to a mosque in 1571. Park your car near the walled city gates and explore the historic square and cathedral on foot.

* **Category**: Historic Site
* **Coordinates**: 35.1250, 33.9428
* **Google Maps**: [View on Google Maps](https://maps.google.com/?q=Lala+Mustafa+Pasa+Mosque,+Famagusta)

**Recommended Stay**:  
45 minutes

### 02:38
**Kapalı Maraş (Varosha)**

A surreal 'ghost town' that was frozen in time for decades. You can now walk or cycle down its streets lined with abandoned mid-century hotels. A premier Mediterranean luxury resort hub in the 1960s, Varosha was fenced off during the 1974 conflict and lay completely abandoned until partially reopening in 2020. Rent a bicycle at the entrance—the paved paths stretch for kilometers.

* **Category**: Museum / Historic Site
* **Coordinates**: 35.1114, 33.9556
* **Google Maps**: [View on Google Maps](https://maps.google.com/?q=Varosha,+Famagusta)

**Recommended Stay**:  
1.5 hours

↓

*10 minutes cycle/walk • 2 km*

### 04:18
**Varosha Beach**

A stunning sandy beach bordering the abandoned towers of Varosha, offering unique clear turquoise waters and a historic backdrop. Once rated among the best beaches in the world and visited by international movie stars. Today it features public walkways, changing rooms, and sun loungers next to history. Enjoy a late afternoon swim and watch the sunset behind the empty high-rises.

* **Category**: Beach
* **Coordinates**: 35.1095, 33.9590
* **Google Maps**: [View on Google Maps](https://maps.google.com/?q=Varosha+Beach,+Famagusta)

**Recommended Stay**:  
2 hours
